About Me

I'm a Knowledge Management and Governance leader with 13+ years of experience designing systems that turn complex information into reliable, accessible, and actionable knowledge. My expertise lies at the intersection of knowledge architecture, regulatory compliance, change enablement, and AI-driven transformation.

I believe effective Knowledge Management goes beyond documentation. It's about building the infrastructure that enables people and AI systems to access trusted information, adapt to change, and operate at scale.
