About

From Content to
Knowledge Systems

My career began in journalism, where I learned to uncover the story in complex information and make it meaningful to an audience. Advertising taught me how to shape narratives to influence perception. Moving into UX helped me understand how people interact with information — what they need, when they need it, and how content can make their experiences simpler.

Over 13+ years, I've brought these perspectives together across global organisations, working with diverse audiences, business contexts, and levels of complexity. At Coinbase, I apply this experience to Knowledge Management and AI-enabled systems, building the content architecture, governance frameworks, and operational processes that make information reliable, accessible, and useful at scale.

My focus is on turning complex information into trusted knowledge — and building the foundations that enable people and AI to put it to work.
