About Me

My career began in journalism, where I learned to uncover the story in complex information and make it meaningful to an audience. Advertising taught me how to shape narratives to influence perception. Moving into UX and content design shifted my focus to how people interact with information and how content can make their experiences simpler and more intuitive.

Over 13+ years, I've brought these perspectives together across global organisations, working with diverse audiences, business contexts, and levels of complexity. Today, at Coinbase, I apply this experience to Knowledge Management and AI-enabled systems, building the content architecture, governance frameworks, and operational processes that make information reliable, accessible, and useful at scale.

I believe effective Knowledge Management goes beyond documentation. It's about building the infrastructure that enables people and AI systems to access trusted information, adapt to change, and operate at scale.
